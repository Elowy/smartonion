# Credits

SmartOnion uses the following third-party assets and libraries. The sounds and music are released under CC0
(public domain dedication; credit is not required, but it is given here with thanks). The font is released under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and the icons under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (credit is required and given
below). The GodotSteam library is released under the MIT licence, which asks for its copyright and permission
notice to ship with every copy (given below).

## Audio

### Sound effects (Kenney, CC0 1.0)

All sound effects come from Kenney's free asset packs by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- Music Jingles (used as short stingers): https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles
- Casino Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- Sci-fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

Some sounds were trimmed or faded out and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

### Music (OpenGameArt, CC0 1.0)

All tracks are released under CC0 1.0 and were converted to Ogg Vorbis (the loop tracks were not edited
otherwise).

- Main menu: "Epic Main Menu Theme ~ Loop" by CleytonKauffman
  https://opengameart.org/content/epic-main-menu-theme-loop
- Combat: "JRPG Epic Rock Battle Theme #1" (loop version) by HydroGene
  https://opengameart.org/content/jrpg-epic-rock-battle-theme-1
- Boss fights: "Boss Battle Theme" (Battle RPG Theme Var) by CleytonKauffman
  https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-theme
- Breaks and market: "Medieval: The Old Tower Inn" (loop version) by RandomMind
  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-old-tower-inn

## Fonts

### Cinzel (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

Titles, headings and buttons are set in Cinzel, Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors
(https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel), released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org (the licence text is in assets/fonts/OFL.txt). The variable font file comes from
the Google Fonts repository: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cinzel

## Icons

### Game-icons.net (Creative Commons Attribution 3.0)

The ability, talent, talent tree, consumable and shrine pictures are icons from https://game-icons.net, released
under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Icons made by Lorc (http://lorcblog.blogspot.com) and Delapouite (https://delapouite.com), available at
https://game-icons.net. The icons were scaled, tinted with the school or item colour and outlined; the
contributors' list and the licence note of the icon archive are in assets/icons/LICENSE.txt.

- Lorc: https://game-icons.net/tags/lorc.html
- Delapouite: https://game-icons.net/tags/delapouite.html
