## Audio

### Sound effects (Kenney, CC0 1.0)

All sound effects come from Kenney's free asset packs by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- Music Jingles (used as short stingers): https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles
- Casino Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- Sci-fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

Some sounds were trimmed or faded out and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

All tracks are released under CC0 1.0 and were converted to Ogg Vorbis (the loop tracks were not edited
